The Panopticon is a location in the Containment Sector of the Oldest House. Founded by Zachariah Trench, it is the Federal Bureau of Control's repository for Altered Items and Objects of Power. The Panopticon has eight labeled floors with hundreds of cells known as crucibles where Altered Items are kept. It connects to the Firebreak, the Archives, Fortified Units, and the P6 Cell. The current Panopticon Supervisor is Frederick Langston.

Hidden Locations[]

  • On the fifth floor of the Panopticon, there is a hole in the wall from Jesse can get into. Inside contains both the Typewritten Page and the Oh Deer Diner Coffee Thermos in their individual Unit cells. When she first enters, Jesse will receive an apparition of Alan Wake, similar to the apparitions she receives from the Hotline. Accessing this area requires the use of the Levitate ability.

Trivia[]

  • The name and basic design of the Panopticon are derived from the work of 18th-century English philosopher and social theorist Jeremy Bentham, who proposed a "panopticon" or "all-seeing" prison in which the cells were arrayed around the inside of a circular facility with a single guard looking out on them from the center. Bentham's idea was that while the guard could not monitor all the prisoners at once, each prisoner would know that they could be under surveillance at any time, and therefore have to act properly at all times. This theory would later be brought to wider attention in the mid-1970s when the French post-modernist philosopher Michel Foucault famously used it as a metaphor for the modern surveillance state. In the FBC version, the same concept serves to ensure that the altered items are stored safely, because (according to Langston) they tend to be more calm when they're being observed.
  • Langston can be heard commenting that the "Sudden Stop hits theaters tonight" and that he "can't believe" he is "missing an Alex Casey movie for this." This is a reference to Alan Wake's final Alex Casey novel, The Sudden Stop.
  • The redacted signage for the third floor appears to read "Remedy Offices" under the censoring stroke.
  • There are two separate Fortified Units labeled 714 and two labeled 715 - one of each on level 1 and one of each on level 4. It is not clear whether this is intended to be the result of some kind of in-universe duplicative effect similar to that of the Waist Mannequin altered item, or just an oversight by the developers.
  • Due to an invisible "ceiling" it is not possible to access the floors above 5 even with the Levitate ability.
  • The Panopticon was constructed sometime during Trench's tenure, as Langston remarks that he was the Director whom put aside funding for it.
